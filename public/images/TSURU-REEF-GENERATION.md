## Water animation

The landing scene combines a lightly displaced copy of the water image with two slow, offset layers of sparse horizontal sun glints from the code-native `reef-water-reflections.svg`. The SVG wave filter uses displacement scale 4 and a gentle 16-second turbulence cycle; the initial stronger effect was reduced at the user's request. A shoreline polygon and feathered vertical mask constrain the effect to the bay on the same image plane as the background, preserving alignment on mobile. The overlays sit behind soldiers, ignore pointer input, and disappear with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Glint animations pause when the location is hidden. No raster artwork or soldier animation is modified.
